NDIS information

Clear information at your pace.

The NDIS provides funding for eligible people with disability to pursue goals and access reasonable and necessary supports.

Getting started

  1. Contact us and ask questions
  2. Discuss goals, preferences and plan funding
  3. Agree to a clear service agreement
  4. Start supports and review them together

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose my provider?

Yes. Choice and control are central to the NDIS.

Can I bring an advocate?

Yes. You may involve a family member, advocate, nominee or interpreter.
